Description of the related research project: NeuroDev is a case-control study that includes a subset of parent controls. Cases are children (2 – 17 years old) with DSM-5 neurodevelopmental disorders, namely any of the following: a) intellectual disability (ID); b) global developmental delay (GDD); c) communication disorders; d) autism spectrum disorders (ASD); e) attention deficit and hyperactivity disorder (ADHD); and f) specific learning disorders (e.g. dyslexia). Cases included affected siblings. Controls are ancestry matched children from the same geographic location. Controls did not include unaffected siblings. All cases and child controls were comprehensively phenotyped (including but not limited to cognition, behavior, dysmorphology, and medical history). Whole blood samples were collected for DNA extraction which was utilized for exome sequencing, genotyping and biobanking. A subset of participants and family controls were consented to generate lymphoblastoid cell lines (LCLs) and induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSC) for banking. In addition, we captured cognition and genetic data from willing biological parents of case children who had the capacity to consent.
